The Game stores certain gameplay and preference information locally on your device. This may include:

* Current level and unlocked levels
* Level completion records
* Stars earned

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide game functionality and preserve your progress.

If you uninstall the Game or clear its application data, locally stored game information may be deleted.
